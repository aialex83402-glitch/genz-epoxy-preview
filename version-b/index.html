Systems

Pick the finish.
We handle the rest.

Coatings and concrete polishing. The right system depends on the slab, the space, and how you use it. Custom quotes only.

  1. 01

    Flake

    Full-broadcast vinyl flake over a pigmented base, locked under a clear polyaspartic topcoat. A common choice for garages. Top sellers are Graphite, Cobalt, and Tuscany.

    Garages · Laundry · Patios
  2. 02

    Metallic

    Hand-worked metallic pigments in a clear epoxy that move like poured stone. Every floor is one of one. Best indoors and out of direct sun.

    Basements · Showrooms · Living areas
  3. 03

    Solid color & polyaspartic

    Clean single-color systems with a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat and optional non-slip aggregate. Shop bay lines can be discussed for the space.

    Shops · Warehouses · Kitchens
  4. 04

    Concrete polishing

    Polished concrete for spaces that want the slab itself as the finish. Diamond grinding is still part of the prep.

    Interior concrete
  5. 05

    Repair & prep

    Diamond grinding on every job, crack and spall repair, and old-coating removal when the slab needs it. Moisture testing is done when needed.

    Any concrete

How we work

Good floors start below the surface.

  1. 1

    Assess

    Moisture testing when needed, plus a look at cracks and the existing surface. You get a custom quote for the system that fits. No minimum job size.

  2. 2

    Grind & repair

    Mechanical diamond grinding on every job opens the concrete so the coating can bond. Cracks, spalls, and control joints are filled and ground flush when the slab needs it.

  3. 3

    Build the system

    Basecoat, then a full-broadcast flake, metallic, solid color, or concrete polishing. A UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat goes on coating systems.

  4. 4

    Return to use

    Foot traffic is often possible about 24 hours after the work, and parking vehicles about 72 hours after. That is separate from the 1–2 day installation. Actual timing depends on the project, conditions, and the crew’s instructions.
