How we work

Good floors start below the surface.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We moisture-test the slab, map cracks and check the old surface. You get a straight answer on which system fits and what it costs.

  2. 2

    Grind & repair

    Diamond grinding opens the concrete so the coating actually bonds. Cracks, spalls and control joints get filled and ground flush.

  3. 3

    Build the system

    Primer, pigmented base, broadcast or metallic layer, then a UV-stable topcoat. Each layer cures before the next goes down.

  4. 4

    Walk on it tomorrow

    Foot traffic in 24 hours, vehicles in 72. We leave you the care sheet and the warranty, and we come back if anything isn't right.
