Our process

Prep & Coating Process

Great epoxy starts with mechanical prep — not just paint over dusty concrete.

1

Mechanical Diamond Grinding

We grind to open the concrete profile for adhesion and to remove weak surface laitance.

2

Crack & Concrete Repair

Cracks, joints, and spalls are addressed so the finished floor stays smooth and uniform.

3

Moisture-Mitigating Basecoat & Full Flake Broadcast

Basecoat goes down, then a full flake broadcast for color, hide, and texture.

4

UV-Stable Polyaspartic Clear Topcoat

A clear topcoat locks flakes in place with a clean satin-gloss finish.
